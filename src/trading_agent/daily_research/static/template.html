Read the metrics correctly

Trailing total return
Gross compounded change over the policy's momentum window, including reviewed share factors and cash distributions. The analytical index assumes reinvestment at the same close, ignoring payment delays, costs and tax. It is historical, not a return forecast.
Trend and rank
A positive trailing return and index above its trailing mean pass the trend rules. Eligible candidates rank by return with deterministic symbol ties.
Daily volatility
Population standard deviation of observed daily gross returns over the momentum window. This is not a promised risk or maximum-loss bound.
Turnover
Mean raw close multiplied by volume. This proxy does not prove executable depth or available liquidity.
