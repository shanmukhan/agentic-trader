Account safety

  • Each purchase, including buy fees, is limited to 25% of current account equity. Existing risk and exposure limits may reduce it further.
  • At most five accepted entry orders per IST day. Cancelled entries use a slot; partial fills and retries of the same order do not add slots. Exits remain available.
  • A 25% fall from observed peak equity pauses new entries until you explicitly reset the reference. Data integrity incidents block entries for the rest of that IST day.
  • Cash only; no borrowing or short selling. Unreadable or inconsistent account records block execution.

This app cannot deposit or withdraw external funds. Contributions and SIPs invest existing cash. Corporate-action income is investment performance. Never edit the financial database to change a balance.
