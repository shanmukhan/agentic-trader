Guided onboarding · your next steps
  1. For Approval/Automatic, open Strategies → Prepare a paper strategy. Download genuine Nifty 100/500 history, review the proposed calendar, basis and policy, then install while disconnected in Manual. The readiness cards explain every remaining blocker; installation never starts trading.
  2. Open Broker & feed setup. In step 3, check the saved broker session. If expired, disconnect any requested feed, complete Zerodha login and exchange the returned request token.
  3. The catalog and selected symbols may already be configured. Step 4 shows their actual tokens and tick sizes.
  4. In step 5, add a genuine reviewed NSE session with the correct date, hours and source. Preparing a future date does not open today's market.
  5. In step 6, connect market data and confirm. A connection is not a guarantee of fresh accepted quotes.
  6. For long-term accounts, open Risk Center and complete current-session price-basis reviews for each supported symbol. Review actual intervening corporate actions; this is not a box to tick blindly.
  7. During the configured entry window of an open reviewed session, wait for fresh accepted quotes and inspect every readiness reason.
  8. Use Purchases & SIP to preview and explicitly confirm a long-term paper purchase. Acceptance needs later usable quotes to fill. The Trading desk can use an explicitly configured reviewed real-data profile. Without it, the current account retains your manual watchlist.

Outside market/entry hours, blocked entries and stale quotes are expected. Connecting after the close cannot create executable fresh market data. Current equity-share fees do not validate ETF execution costs.
