Your next steps

  1. Open Broker & feed setup. In step 3, check the saved broker session. If expired, disconnect any requested feed, complete Zerodha login and exchange the returned request token.
  2. The catalog and selected symbols may already be configured. Step 4 shows their actual tokens and tick sizes.
  3. In step 5, add a genuine reviewed NSE session with the correct date, hours and source. Preparing a future date does not open today's market.
  4. In step 6, connect market data and confirm. A connection is not a guarantee of fresh accepted quotes.
  5. For long-term accounts, open Trading console and complete current-session price-basis reviews for each supported symbol. Review actual intervening corporate actions; this is not a box to tick blindly.
  6. During the configured entry window of an open reviewed session, wait for fresh accepted quotes and inspect every readiness reason.
  7. Use Purchases & SIP to preview and explicitly confirm a long-term paper purchase. Acceptance needs later usable quotes to fill. Automatic broad-market stock selection is not yet implemented.

Outside market/entry hours, blocked entries and stale quotes are expected. Connecting after the close cannot create executable fresh market data. Current equity-share fees do not validate ETF execution costs.
