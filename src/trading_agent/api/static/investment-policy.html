Choose months or years and monthly or quarterly reviews. Every choice starts blank. Drafts are research plans: execution, installation and backtesting for this policy family are unavailable.

What a draft changes

Saving records your choices in separate research storage. Your installed strategy, paper mode, holdings and broker connection stay as configured. The legacy daily strategy keeps its existing holding-session limit.

    1. Build your policy draft

    Work through four groups in any order. You can review and save an incomplete draft; empty fields stay unresolved. These are your research choices, not recommended limits.

    2. Saved draft snapshots

    Saving creates an immutable snapshot. Editing a copy creates another snapshot when its content changes. Reading or refreshing drafts grants no account authority.

    Calendar preview and technical identity

    Calendar-month horizons keep the original day where possible and clamp to the last calendar day when needed. Exchange review dates require an explicitly reviewed complete calendar; weekdays and partial session lists cannot establish a review date. Missing cycles are skipped rather than replayed.

    Use .venv/bin/trading-agent investment-policy --help to inspect a draft file or preview review dates from a local calendar declaration. This produces a research report without changing an account.

    Semantic identity excludes the draft name. Saving binds the complete reviewed snapshot, including its name. No active policy or experiment identity is inferred from a draft alone.
