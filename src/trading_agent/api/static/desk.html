Paper trading mode

Manual

You decide what to buy. Use the purchase form or advanced controls.

Approval · Semi-autonomous

The configured strategy proposes buys and discretionary sells. You review the reason and limits, then approve or decline each proposal.

Automatic · Autonomous paper trades

The configured strategy chooses and submits eligible paper orders without per-trade approval. Independent data, risk and cost limits still apply.

Choose Manual to pause strategy entries. Locking or closing this tab leaves the server running. Required protective exits do not wait for approval.

What happens when a mode changes or pauses?

Changing mode cancels this desk’s unfilled buy remainder and outstanding proposals; it does not sell held shares. Stop/target, kill-switch and required protective exits remain independent. Restart, a new session, feed loss or a risk stop requires explicit activation again.

    Sources, coverage and exclusions

    2. Choose a policy and finish

    One review handles installation, the ranked shortlist, eligible price-basis reviews for today and the market-data connection. We show the exact stocks and calendar changes before you confirm.

    Policy rules and limitations

    Rules: positive 20-session momentum, 50-session trend, average daily turnover ≥ ₹1 crore and daily volatility ≤ 5%. Long-term entries use a 2% stop, 4% target, ≤ 1% entry gap and a 20-session holding limit. Intraday uses completed five-minute opening-range-breakout signals after warmup. Fees, cash, spread and risk limits can reject any proposal.

    No ML model is invented or silently replaced with rules. Current constituents cannot train or validate a historical strategy. Supported stocks with a returned corporate action in the history interval are excluded instead of guessing adjusted prices.
