Manual

Approval · Semi-autonomous

The configured strategy proposes buys and discretionary sells. You review the reason and limits, then approve or decline each proposal.

Automatic · Autonomous paper trades

The configured strategy chooses and submits eligible paper orders without per-trade approval. Independent data, risk and cost limits still apply.

Stop/target, kill-switch and required protective exits do not wait for approval. Changing mode cancels this desk's unfilled buy remainder and outstanding proposals; it does not sell held shares. Restart, a new session, feed loss or a risk stop requires explicit activation again. Locking or closing this browser tab does not pause the server; choose Manual to stop strategy-driven entries.

    Which stocks does this account analyse?

    Discovery may cover up to 500 reviewed symbols; execution is limited to a shortlist of at most ten, including every held symbol. Applying it requires manual mode, a disconnected feed and no pending orders.

    Screening evidence and exclusions

    Screen strength and a model score are not guaranteed profit or an expected-return forecast. Rules-only policies are experimental paper hypotheses. Intraday needs completed five-minute signals; long-term needs prior completed daily information. Each horizon uses a separate account and capital.
