    Prepare a paper strategy

    Not prepared

    Download genuine history, review the evidence, then install a strategy. You can do this after market hours. Installation leaves Manual selected; starting Approval or Automatic is a separate confirmation during a usable trading session.

    1. Download real data

    Use current Nifty 100 or Nifty 500 constituents, rather than your old five-stock list. We match broker tokens, read the official holiday/action sources and download completed daily candles from your saved Kite session.

    History needs a paid/entitled broker data session. Reads are paced at least 400 ms apart; larger runs can take several minutes. Corporate-action and incomplete-history exclusions may substantially reduce coverage. Downloading does not change your feed selection.

    Downloaded sources and stock exclusions

    2. Review and install

    The calendar below is proposed from the downloaded holiday list and normal hours. Review date completeness, special-session notices and the corporate-action exclusions. No review is pre-approved.

    Review the full history period and displayed future dates. Correct hours if needed. Additional/missing sessions require fresh complete source data; never use evening hours to simulate an open market.

    Rules: positive 20-session momentum, 50-session trend, average daily turnover ≥ ₹1 crore and daily volatility ≤ 5%. Long-term entries use a 2% stop, 4% target, ≤ 1% entry gap and a 20-session holding limit. Intraday uses completed five-minute opening-range-breakout signals after warmup. Fees, cash, spread and risk limits can reject any proposal.

    Current constituent snapshots cannot train or validate a historical strategy. No ML model is invented or silently replaced with rules. Supported stocks with any returned corporate action in the history interval are excluded instead of guessing adjusted prices. Each account has its own horizon, strategy and virtual cash.

    Which stocks does this account analyse?

    Discovery may cover up to 500 reviewed symbols; execution is limited to a shortlist of at most ten, including every held symbol. Applying it requires manual mode, a disconnected feed and no pending orders.

    Screening evidence and exclusions

    Screen strength and a model score are not guaranteed profit or an expected-return forecast. Rules-only policies are experimental paper hypotheses. Intraday needs completed five-minute signals; long-term needs prior completed daily information. Each horizon uses a separate account and capital.
